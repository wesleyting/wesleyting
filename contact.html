Contact

Need help with a website?

Send a note about the site, storefront, page, or technical issue you want help with. I am open to frontend, Shopify, ecommerce, and web production work.

Start with the basics.

Email directly at wesleytingdev@gmail.com, or use the form. The form is ready for Netlify Forms and will submit once the site is deployed on Netlify.

Useful requests to send through.

Shopify help

Product pages, landing pages, storefront updates, theme fixes, launch support, and ecommerce maintenance.

Frontend work

Responsive page builds, design implementation, animation support, UI polish, and technical cleanup.

Existing websites

Performance issues, broken layouts, mobile problems, tracking setup, form issues, or general site improvements.

What To Include

A few details make it easier to respond with useful next steps.

  • The website or store URL, if one already exists.
  • What you need help with: new build, update, bug fix, redesign, launch, or ongoing support.
  • Any platform details, such as Shopify, WordPress, custom frontend, or another CMS.
  • Timeline, budget range, and whether the work is urgent or flexible.
  • Links to designs, references, product pages, or examples of the issue.
  • The best way to follow up if email is not preferred.
