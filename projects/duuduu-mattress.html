DuuDuu Mattress

Shopify development for an Australian mattress brand focused on product education, clear purchasing paths, and conversion-focused storefront updates.

Product pages, landing pages, and ongoing Shopify support for a mattress brand.

DuuDuu Mattress sells mattresses and sleep products in Australia. The storefront needs to explain product benefits clearly, support confident buying decisions, and keep ecommerce updates moving without slowing down marketing or operations.

Shopify Liquid Ecommerce

Overview

Clearer product storytelling for a purchase that requires trust.

Challenge

Mattress shoppers need useful information before they buy: materials, comfort, delivery, returns, and reasons to trust the brand. The site has to present that information without making the buying path feel heavy.

Approach

I focused the draft structure around practical Shopify work: product page improvements, landing page sections, reusable content blocks, and storefront updates that can support campaigns and ongoing ecommerce needs.

Work Delivered

Practical ecommerce improvements across the storefront.

  • Product page layout support for feature explanations, buying prompts, and customer decision points.
  • Landing page sections for promotional campaigns, product education, and conversion-focused storytelling.
  • Responsive QA across mobile and desktop so key purchase information remains readable and usable.
  • Shopify theme updates using Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Ongoing content and layout support for storefront changes after launch.
  • Technical troubleshooting for ecommerce sections, visual issues, and front-end behavior.

Outcome

A storefront foundation that supports product education and day-to-day ecommerce work.

Business Value

The site structure supports a clearer path from product interest to purchase, while giving the brand room to launch campaigns, update content, and improve pages over time.

Stack

Shopify, Liquid, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive development, ecommerce QA, and storefront maintenance.
