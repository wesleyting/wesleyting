VegasPaulyC

Turning a creator-led audience into an owned ecommerce operation.

VegasPaulyC had more than one million followers across TikTok and Instagram but no dedicated sales channel. I built the brand’s first Shopify storefront and connected the product, preorder, support, and fulfilment workflows behind it.

My Role

More Than a Website Build

This project required more than a typical developer role.

I was responsible for connecting the storefront, products, customer support, suppliers, and fulfillment workflow into one working system. When something needed to be set up, fixed, coordinated, or clarified, I was usually the person figuring out how to move it forward.

The goal was not just to launch a website, but to build a setup stable enough for the business to keep running without constant manual attention.

Launch

From Product to Launch

We launched custom sound buttons while production, international shipping, and warehouse timing were still in motion. The product moved from China to a fulfilment partner in Florida before reaching customers internationally.

I built the preorder page and set clear expectations around timing, then coordinated updates and fulfilment preparation as inventory moved from supplier to warehouse.
