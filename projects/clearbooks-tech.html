Clearbooks Tech

A professional services website for accounting and CFO support, built around trust, clear service communication, and client inquiry flow.

A practical website foundation for accounting and CFO services.

Clearbooks Tech provides accounting and CFO services for businesses that need financial clarity and operational support. The website needs to communicate services clearly, make the business feel credible, and guide visitors toward booking a conversation.

CMS Deployment Calendly

Overview

Professional service websites need clarity before persuasion.

Challenge

Accounting and CFO services are trust-based. Visitors need to understand what the firm offers, who it helps, how to take the next step, and why the business is credible.

Approach

The draft structure emphasizes straightforward service pages, editable content, clear calls to action, and a booking path that supports discovery calls without adding friction.

Work Delivered

Website structure for services, content management, and lead capture.

  • Service-focused page structure for accounting, CFO support, advisory, and business operations content.
  • CMS-ready content sections so the business can update messaging and service details over time.
  • Calendly integration planning for a direct inquiry and consultation booking path.
  • Responsive development for clear reading and navigation across desktop and mobile.
  • Deployment workflow support for publishing updates with fewer manual steps.
  • Technical QA for layout consistency, link behavior, and content presentation.

Outcome

A clear services website that supports credibility and client inquiries.

Business Value

The website gives the business a practical foundation for explaining services, managing content, and moving interested visitors toward a scheduled conversation.

Stack

CMS structure, responsive frontend development, deployment workflow, Calendly integration, content planning, and technical QA.
