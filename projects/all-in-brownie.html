The $10,000 Naming Competition

Frankie asked his audience to name the brownie, and the $10,000 prize was split among everyone who submitted the selected name. I built the responsive Next.js entry site, including validation, eligibility checks, consent capture, and structured submissions for review.

Multi-Brand

Building BMF Brownie for Cowboy Cerrone

BMF Brownie took the same core product into a separate brand built around Cowboy Cerrone. I developed Cowboy Cerrone’s Shopify storefront and adapted the product presentation, packaging, and messaging for the BMF audience.

Outcome

One product, two brands, three builds.

The project moved from a fan-led naming competition into a subscription-ready All-In storefront, then into a separate BMF Brownie experience for Cowboy Cerrone. Each build supported a different part of the launch while keeping both brands distinct.
