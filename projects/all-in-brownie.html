All-In Brownie

A $10,000 naming competition built into an ecommerce launch.

I developed the Next.js competition site, the entry and consent flow, and the Shopify storefront for All-In Brownie. The same product later launched as BMF Brownie with a separate identity and storefront.

Competition Site

A $10,000 competition with a real entry system.

I built the responsive Next.js site that collected submissions, enforced entry rules, recorded consent, and gave the team consistent data to review. GSAP handled the motion. Validation and privacy handling kept the entries usable.

  • FrontendNext.js
  • InteractionGSAP
  • Entry rulesValidation
  • EligibilityConsent

Ecommerce

From campaign traffic to checkout.

I carried the launch into Shopify, working across the storefront, product detail, subscriptions, and the purchase path. The competition site and store used different stacks but functioned as one customer journey.

Results

Sales backed by customer proof.

The reported performance data covers more than the launch itself: $24K+ in sales, 377 orders, 677 units sold, and nearly 32,000 store sessions. Reviews from the poker and fitness communities gave buyers proof beyond the brand’s own claims.
